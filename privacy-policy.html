Privacy Policy for Shellby

Effective Date: October 2026

Introduction

Shellby ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Key Point: Minimal Data Collection. Shellby has no accounts and keeps your recipes, favorites and lists on your device. The only data our own server receives is what it needs to check a purchase with Apple or Google (see "In-App Purchases" below).

COPPA Compliance

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our age-gate system prevents minors from accessing features that involve data processing. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Information We Collect

1. Local Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage:

  • Favorite recipes
  • Shopping list items
  • App settings and preferences
  • Recipe viewing history
  • Premium purchase status

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Export feature. Your premium status is the one exception: see the next section.

2. In-App Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We never see your name, payment card or store account.

When you buy or restore Premium, the app sends the purchase to our verification server at api.shellby.app, which asks Apple or Google whether it is genuine. Each request contains:

  • the product you bought, and the purchase or transaction ID
  • the store's signed purchase record (Apple) or purchase token (Google)
  • a random install ID the app creates once (not linked to your device, name or store account)
  • on supported devices, a device-integrity token from Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity, used to check that the request comes from the real app
  • your IP address, which every internet request carries

The server keeps a short record so one purchase can't be copied to many devices: a one-way hash of the purchase ID (never the ID or token itself), the install ID, the product, and when it was first and last seen. These records are deleted 12 months after their last use. Server logs, which include IP addresses, are kept for up to 30 days. Nothing else is stored, and no account is created.

3. Analytics

If you have confirmed adult age (13+) via our age screen, anonymized analytics data is sent via Firebase Analytics to help us improve the app. You can disable this at any time in Settings → Privacy. No analytics data is collected from users under 13.

4. Crash Reporting

If the app crashes, anonymized crash data may be sent via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs. This is enabled only after you confirm you are 13 or older. No crash data is collected from users under 13.

5. Photos and Fonts

Recipe photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons, and some display fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts the first time they are used. These services receive your IP address, as any website you open would; the app sends them nothing else.

How We Use Your Information

  1. App Functionality: To provide recipe browsing, cooking mode, and timer features
  2. Favorites & Shopping List: To save your preferences locally
  3. Purchase Verification: To check purchases with Apple or Google and stop one purchase from being copied to many devices
  4. Crash Reporting: To identify and fix bugs
  5. Analytics (opt-in): To improve app features and performance

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Advertising (Google AdMob) and analytics are only enabled for users who have confirmed adult age; no ads or tracking are served to users under 13. Data may be processed by:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob for adult-confirmed users; Google Play, to verify Android purchases; Google Fonts)
  • Apple (App Store, to verify iOS purchases)
  • Fly.io, which hosts our verification server
  • Neon, which hosts the database for the purchase records (in Germany)
  • Wikimedia Commons, for recipe photos

These providers have their own privacy policies and data processing practices.

Data Security

Apart from the purchase records described above, your data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your favorites and settings are as secure as your device
  • We recommend using device lock screen security
  • Backups are your responsibility via the Export feature

Local storage is encrypted using AES-256 encryption with keys stored in the platform keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Sensitive data shared with home-screen widgets (such as your shopping list) is encrypted before it leaves the app's encrypted storage.

Exported backups are not encrypted by the app. They are created only when you choose the Export feature and are your responsibility to store securely.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Export Your Data: Use the "Export Data" feature in Settings to create a JSON backup of your favorites and settings.
  • Delete Your Data: Use "Clear all data" in Settings → Privacy to remove your locally stored data, or uninstall the app. Your age confirmation and purchase entitlement are kept so the app does not re-ask for them or degrade a paid plan.
  • Withdraw Consent: You can disable analytics tracking at any time in Settings.
  • Purchase Records: Because there is no account, we can't tell which server record is yours. To have it deleted, email privacy@shellby.app with the order or transaction ID from your Apple or Google receipt, and we will delete the matching record.

European Users (GDPR) and California Residents (CCPA)

Agizy LLC, the publisher of Shellby, is the controller of the data described here. We process it on these legal bases:

  • Contract: checking a purchase so we can deliver what you bought
  • Legitimate interests: preventing one purchase from being copied to many devices, and keeping the server secure (logs)
  • Consent: analytics, which you can turn off in Settings

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, email privacy@shellby.app. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

Children's Privacy

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our age-gate system:

  • Asks for birth month and year (a neutral age screen) the first time you use a feature that needs it, such as cooking along, sharing or Premium, and always before analytics, crash reporting, advertising or a new purchase begins. Restoring an earlier purchase from Settings checks it with our server without the age screen, because the store requires restore to stay available; that check sends only the purchase details listed under "In-App Purchases"
  • Blocks analytics, advertising, tracking, and crash reporting for users under 13
  • Restricts social and paid features (discovery challenges, family sync, purchases) for users under 13 with a parental-gate placeholder
  • Never collects personal information from minors

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with data, contact us immediately at privacy@shellby.app.

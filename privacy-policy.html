Privacy Policy for Shellby

Effective Date: September 2026

Introduction

Shellby ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Key Point: Minimal Data Collection. Shellby operates with minimal data collection. We do not operate a backend server that stores your personal data.

COPPA Compliance

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our age-gate system prevents minors from accessing features that involve data processing. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Information We Collect

1. Local Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage:

  • Favorite recipes
  • Shopping list items
  • App settings and preferences
  • Recipe viewing history
  • Premium purchase status

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the Export feature.

2. In-App Purchases

When you make a purchase, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). Only a receipt token is used locally to verify your purchase status. No payment information is handled or stored by our app.

3. Analytics

If you have confirmed adult age (13+) via our age screen, anonymized analytics data is sent via Firebase Analytics to help us improve the app. You can disable this at any time in Settings → Privacy. No analytics data is collected from users under 13.

4. Crash Reporting

If the app crashes, anonymized crash data may be sent via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs. This is enabled only after you confirm you are 13 or older. No crash data is collected from users under 13.

How We Use Your Information

  1. App Functionality: To provide recipe browsing, cooking mode, and timer features
  2. Favorites & Shopping List: To save your preferences locally
  3. Purchase Verification: To unlock premium features via Apple/Google receipt
  4. Crash Reporting: To identify and fix bugs
  5. Analytics (opt-in): To improve app features and performance

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Advertising (Google AdMob) and analytics are only enabled for users who have confirmed adult age; no ads or tracking are served to users under 13. Data may be processed by:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob for adult-confirmed users)
  • Apple (App Store, for iOS users)

These providers have their own privacy policies and data processing practices.

Data Security

Since all personal data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your favorites and settings are as secure as your device
  • We recommend using device lock screen security
  • Backups are your responsibility via the Export feature

Local storage is encrypted using AES-256 encryption with keys stored in the platform keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Sensitive data shared with home-screen widgets (such as your shopping list) is encrypted before it leaves the app's encrypted storage.

Exported backups are not encrypted by the app. They are created only when you choose the Export feature and are your responsibility to store securely.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Export Your Data: Use the "Export Data" feature in Settings to

create a JSON backup of your favorites and settings.

  • Delete Your Data: Use "Clear all data" in Settings → Privacy to

remove your locally stored data, or uninstall the app. Your age confirmation and purchase entitlement are kept so the app does not re-ask for them or degrade a paid plan.

  • Withdraw Consent: You can disable analytics tracking at any time in

Settings.

Children's Privacy

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our age-gate system:

  • Asks for birth month and year (a neutral age screen) before any data

collection, advertising, or personalized content begins

  • Blocks analytics, advertising, tracking, and crash reporting for users under 13
  • Restricts social and paid features (discovery challenges, family sync,

purchases) for users under 13 with a parental-gate placeholder

  • Never collects personal information from minors

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with data, contact us immediately at privacy@shellby.app.
